DragOn Loot

Loot, encounters and party tools for your D&D table.

Download for Android
Version 1.0.3 · Android APK · about 23 MB

What it does

  • The Dungeon Master opens a a room for players on the same WiFi to more easily manage their game. No internet needed.
  • Over 400 monsters with D&D hit points and armor class, rolled initiative, turn order, damage, healing and conditions.
  • Loot drops from the Creature Loot rules, with descriptions that unlock based on each player's tools or skills.
  • Lotting, selling and managing your inventory, with the Dungeon Master able to see and approve every change.
  • Full character sheets, party chat and whispering.

How to install

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download for Android above.
  2. Open the downloaded DragOnLoot.apk (from your notifications or the Downloads app).
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  4. Open DragOn Loot. The first time you host or join a room, allow it to find devices on your network.

Android may warn that the app isn't from the Play Store. That's expected for apps downloaded straight from a website. To update later, download the new version here and install it over the old one; your phone keeps the previous versions data. (designed that way but app is still work in progress)
